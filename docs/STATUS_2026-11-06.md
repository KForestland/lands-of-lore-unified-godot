# LoL2 in Godot: status report

Date: 2026-11-06

This note describes the local working project. The code in this repository is still an older public snapshot
(September 2026); the work below is not published here yet. Previous report: [2026-10-31](STATUS_2026-10-31.md).

## Where it stands (Classic edition)

| Part | Done | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Maps / world | ~93% | All 15 levels built and connected. Every guide map point in 14 areas checked against the original data and the longplay. The renderer now draws only what can be seen through doorways and openings, like the original engine; that fixed most clipping (owner, r16: Huline Jungle "much better"). Open: the Huline Temple interior. |
| Act 1 | ~92% | Playable start to finish; certified as one campaign chain; owner playtests r14 and r16. |
| Act 2 story | ~75% | Steps 7-65 built and tested; the first Huline Temple visit must play its scene on walk-in (fix in the next build). |
| Act 3 story | ~25% | 4 of 28 steps built; all 28 drafted as one save chain to the final scene, under review. |
| Good/evil branches | drafted | Both outcomes of every branch drafted. |
| Items | ~25% | All 170 original items placed and carriable; effects hosted one by one. |
| RPG, dialog, combat, magic, sound | ~10-20% | Act 1 mostly; these come after the main chain. |

**Overall: about 60% of a complete Classic LoL2.**

## What changed since 2026-10-31

- Portal visibility (draw only regions reached through region edges inside the view) for the Temple, Hive, Jungle and
  several Act 2-3 levels.
- Water surfaces for every water region; drowning in deep water (~15 s, measured in the original).
- Act 1 missing beats: the Museum dragon picture click, the Hive pit lift, lever and talk prompts.
- My Journal: notes for stuck points in Acts 1-3; Atlas improvements drafted (explored-only, one floor at a time).
- Act 3: the Chamber of Voices to the city, the arrival lift, the greenhouse fireball, the dry fountain.

## Order of work (owner, 2026-11-06)

Maps -> the main quest chain complete A to Z -> refinement (edge cases, scene order, puzzle nuance, items, RPG,
dialog, sound). A passing test is not a finished area: the owner's playtest clears it.

## Estimated time (rough, not a deadline)

- Story playable start to finish: mid-December 2026.
- Items, RPG, dialog and sound game-wide: February-March 2027.
